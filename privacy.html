Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 18 August 2026

Welcome to FuelMath.net ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how information is handled when you use our website. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, use, and protection for users globally, and complies with major privacy frameworks including the GDPR and CCPA.

Your Calculator Data is Private

We believe in privacy by design for our core tools. Any data you enter into our calculators (e.g., pressures, volumes, dimensions, or weights) is processed entirely locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript. This input data is never transmitted to, stored on, or accessed by our servers.

Advertising and Third-Party Cookies

To keep our tools free and accessible, we display advertisements on our website. We use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website.

These companies may use information (not including your name, address, email address, or telephone number) about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you. This is typically done via the use of cookies or web beacons.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site.
  • Google’s use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy.

Passive Information Collection (Server Logs)

While our calculators run locally, our website hosting infrastructure automatically collects basic, non-personal technical data to ensure security, prevent abuse, and maintain service performance. This "passive" data may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address (often anonymised)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Date and time of access
  • Referring website address

Other Third-Party Services

We utilise minimal third-party services to deliver the website efficiently. These providers have their own privacy policies:

  • Hosting: The website is hosted on Cloudflare. Cloudflare may collect standard server log data as described in their Privacy Policy.
  • Content Delivery Network (CDN): We may use CDNs (such as Tailwind CSS or similar) for styling and performance. Requests to these CDNs may log standard metadata under their respective privacy policies.

Your Global Privacy Rights and Opt-Outs

Depending on your location, you have specific rights regarding your data and advertising preferences:

  • Ad Personalisation Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) Opt-Out page or the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA) page.
  • GDPR (EEA/UK): You have the right to access, rectify, or request the deletion of your personal data. You also have the right to withdraw consent for cookie usage at any time via our cookie consent banner (if applicable).
  • CCPA (California, USA): California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect the website against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect the service, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for general audiences, including students and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such data, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements (including updates to AdSense policies). The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
