Privacy Policy

The Short Version

FuelMath.net collects nothing. All calculations run locally in your browser.

No Data Collection

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels
  • No user accounts, no forms, no newsletter sign-ups
  • Inputs you enter (pressures, volumes, prices) are processed in-browser JavaScript and never transmitted anywhere

Third Parties

Styling uses the Tailwind CSS CDN, which may log standard CDN request metadata (IP address) under their own privacy policy. No other third-party services are embedded.
