Methodology & Formula

Carbon tax exposure is simply emissions × carbon price — but for planning you need it projected over multiple years, with emissions changing as your business grows or your reduction programs take effect.

Year 1 Liability

Cost₁ = Emissions × Carbon Price

Annual Emission Trajectory

Emissionsn+1 = Emissionsn × (1 + Growth%)

Cumulative Liability

Total = Σ (Emissionsn × Carbon Price), n = 1…Years

  • Annual emissions = total Scope 1 CO₂e in tonnes — derive this from the Fuel Combustion CO₂ Calculator
  • Carbon price = the applicable tax or ETS allowance price ($/t CO₂e)
  • Growth = expected change in emissions per year — positive for expansion, negative for decarbonization programs
  • This model holds the carbon price constant; for rising-price scenarios, run the tool at several price points to bracket the range

Use Shadow Pricing for Investment Decisions

Many companies apply an internal "shadow" carbon price (often $50–150/t) to capital projects even where no mandatory price exists today — anticipating future regulation. Running this tool at your shadow price shows how a new boiler, fleet, or plant would perform under plausible future carbon costs.

Reference: World Bank Carbon Pricing Dashboard Methodology

The World Bank tracks carbon pricing instruments (taxes and emissions trading systems) across all jurisdictions, providing the benchmark prices used for exposure modeling. Carbon tax liability = verified emissions × applicable price is the standard formula applied in every carbon-pricing regime worldwide.
