Methodology & Formula

A pump price is a stack of components. Excise duty is a fixed per-litre tax added to the fuel cost, and VAT is then applied as a percentage on the whole pre-VAT total — including the excise. This creates the "tax on tax" effect.

Pre-VAT Total

Subtotal = Base + Freight + Margin + Excise

VAT Amount & Pump Price

VAT = Subtotal × (Rate / 100)  •  Pump = Subtotal + VAT

Total Tax Share

Tax% = (Excise + VAT) / Pump × 100

  • Base price = refinery / wholesale commodity cost per litre
  • Freight = transport and distribution cost; Margin = wholesaler + dealer profit
  • Excise = fixed government duty per litre (not a percentage)
  • VAT = ad valorem tax on the subtotal — so you pay VAT on the excise duty itself

The "Tax on Tax" Effect

Because VAT is calculated on the excise-inclusive subtotal, a portion of the VAT you pay is itself tax on the excise duty. With a 20% VAT and a large excise component, this can add several percent to the effective tax burden beyond what the headline excise rate suggests — a key point in fuel-tax policy debates.
