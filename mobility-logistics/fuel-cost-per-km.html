Methodology & Formula

The cost-per-distance metric is the single most useful number for vehicle economics — it powers delivery pricing, reimbursement policies, fleet budgeting, and fuel-switching comparisons. It normalizes fuel price and efficiency into one directly comparable figure.

Cost per Kilometer

Ckm = Price / Mileage (km/L)

Cost per Mile

Cmile = Ckm × 1.60934

  • Price = Fuel unit price ($/L, $/kg for CNG, or $/kWh equivalent for EVs)
  • Mileage = Distance per unit of fuel (km/L or km/kg)
  • 1 mile = 1.60934 km
  • Common applications: courier per-km pricing, employee mileage reimbursement, fleet fuel budgeting, and comparing fuel types on an equal basis

Fuel Cost vs. Total Cost

This metric captures fuel only. Total operating cost per km also includes maintenance, tyres, insurance, depreciation, and driver costs — typically adding 50–100% on top of the fuel figure for commercial vehicles.
