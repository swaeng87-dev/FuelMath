Methodology & Formula

Misfueling recovery cost is the sum of labour, replacement parts, hazardous-waste disposal, and the wasted fuel itself. The dominant variable is whether the engine was started — running the wrong fuel circulates it through pumps, lines, and injectors, escalating the job from a simple drain to component repair.

Total Recovery Cost

Total = Clabour + Parts + Disposal + Cfuel

  • Engine NOT started — tank drain, line flush, and filter change only (lowest cost)
  • Engine started / driven — fuel has circulated; add inspection and likely replacement of fuel pump, injectors, or high-pressure components
  • Petrol in diesel — most severe: petrol is a solvent that destroys the lubrication diesel injection systems rely on
  • Diesel in petrol — less severe: usually resolved with drain, flush, and spark-plug cleaning, since diesel won't ignite in a petrol engine

Immediate Action Steps

1) Do not start the engine or turn the ignition to "on" (primes the fuel pump). 2) Put the car in neutral and move it clear of the pumps. 3) Call a mobile drain service or have it towed. Acting before starting can keep the bill to a drain-and-flush rather than a full injection-system rebuild.
