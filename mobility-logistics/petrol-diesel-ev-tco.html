Methodology & Formula

Total Cost of Ownership analysis reveals that purchase price is only part of the story. EVs carry higher upfront costs but dramatically lower running costs; diesel offers fuel economy but higher maintenance; petrol sits between. This tool sums capital, energy, and maintenance across your ownership horizon to identify the true cheapest option.

Total Cost of Ownership

TCO = Capital + Energy + Maintenance

Lifetime Energy Cost

Energy = (Annual km / Efficiency) × Unit Price × Years

Lifetime Maintenance

Maintenance = Annual Maint. × Years

  • Capital = Vehicle purchase price; Efficiency = km/L (petrol/diesel) or km/kWh (EV)
  • Unit Price = Fuel price ($/L) or electricity price ($/kWh)
  • EV maintenance is typically 50–70% lower than ICE vehicles — fewer moving parts, no oil changes, regenerative braking reduces brake wear
  • Higher annual mileage shifts the break-even in favor of EVs and diesels; low-mileage drivers may never recover the EV premium

What This Model Excludes

This TCO model captures capital, energy, and maintenance only. Full lifecycle assessments should also include: financing interest, insurance differentials, residual/resale value (EVs depreciate differently), home charger installation cost, and government incentives or road-tax differences. These vary significantly by market.

Reference: Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Standard Lifecycle Assessment

TCO lifecycle assessment is the standard methodology used by fleet managers, leasing companies, and automotive analysts to compare vehicles on a full-cost basis rather than sticker price. The approach is endorsed by fleet industry bodies and is the basis of most corporate vehicle procurement decisions.
