Methodology & Formula

Diesel generator sets are among the most expensive electricity sources in commercial buildings — typically 3–5× grid tariff rates. The true cost has two components: fuel consumption (dominant, ~85–90% of total) and operation & maintenance (lube oil, filters, scheduled overhauls, and capital depreciation).

Fuel Cost per kWh

Cfuel = Pdiesel × SFC

Total Generation Cost

Ctotal = Cfuel + CO&M

  • Pdiesel = Diesel price ($/litre), SFC = Specific fuel consumption (L/kWh)
  • SFC worsens dramatically at low load factors — a DG set running at 25% load burns ~50% more fuel per kWh than at 75% load
  • Oversized DG sets for buildings with variable demand are a major source of hidden energy cost

Reference Standard: ISO 8528

ISO 8528 governs reciprocating internal combustion engine driven AC generating sets, defining rating classes (Emergency, Prime, Continuous), performance tolerance, and test methods. SFC values in this tool reflect ISO 8528-compliant modern DG sets; older or poorly maintained units may exceed these figures by 15–30%.
