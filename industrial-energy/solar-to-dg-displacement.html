Methodology & Formula

When rooftop solar PV operates in parallel with a diesel generator, solar output directly offsets DG runtime. The displaced diesel volume equals solar generation multiplied by the generator's specific fuel consumption — making diesel-heavy sites (remote facilities, islands, industrial parks with frequent outages) the strongest solar economics candidates.

Step 1 — Daily Solar Generation

Eday = Capacity × Sun Hours × PR

Step 2 — Daily Diesel Displaced

Vdiesel = Eday × SFC

Step 3 — Annual Savings & Payback

Savingsyr = Vdiesel × Pdiesel × 365  •  Payback = CAPEX / Savingsyr

  • Capacity = Installed solar capacity (kWp), PR = Performance ratio (losses from inverter, wiring, soiling, temperature)
  • Peak Sun Hours: 3.5–4.0 (temperate), 4.5–5.5 (tropical/subtropical), 5.5–6.0 (desert regions)
  • SFC = DG specific fuel consumption (default 0.25 L/kWh ≈ 75% load)
  • Solar displacing diesel typically pays back in 3–5 years — far faster than solar displacing grid power

Reference: Hybrid Microgrid Fuel Displacement Methodology

This methodology follows standard hybrid microgrid feasibility analysis. For sites with minimum DG loading constraints (DG sets cannot run below ~30% load), a battery buffer or solar controller with DG modulation is recommended to maximize displacement. This calculator assumes 100% of solar output successfully displaces DG generation.
