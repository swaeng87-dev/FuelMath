Methodology & Formula

Boiler fuel consumption is determined by the thermal duty (useful steam energy output) divided by the product of fuel energy content and combustion efficiency. Flue-gas heat loss — carried away by hot exhaust gases — is the dominant efficiency loss in fired steam generators.

Step 1 — Total Thermal Energy Required

E = Duty (kW) × 3.6 × hours  [MJ]

Step 2 — Fuel Consumption

m = E / (GCV × η)

Step 3 — Operating Cost

C = m × Price

  • Duty = Steam thermal output (kW) — 1 tonne steam/hr ≈ 700 kW
  • η = Boiler thermal efficiency (fraction). Flue-gas loss = (1 − η) of fuel input
  • GCV = Gross calorific value per fuel unit (MJ/kg, MJ/L, or MJ/SCM)
  • Natural gas boilers run 2–5% higher efficiency than liquid fuel boilers due to lower excess-air requirements and no unburned carbon losses

Reference Standard: ASME PTC 4 (Fired Steam Generators)

ASME PTC 4 defines the accepted test codes for determining steam generator efficiency, including both the input-output method and the heat-loss method. For operational estimates, the efficiency values above represent typical well-maintained industrial boilers; actual efficiency should be verified via periodic flue-gas analysis (O₂, CO, stack temperature).
