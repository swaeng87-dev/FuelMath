Methodology & Formula

Industrial fuel switching decisions must compare delivered useful heat, not raw fuel volumes. LPG has a higher energy density per kilogram than PNG does per cubic metre, so the equivalent PNG volume must be computed on an energy-equivalent basis before comparing costs.

Step 1 — Useful Heat from Current LPG

E = mLPG × GCVLPG × ηLPG

Step 2 — Equivalent PNG Volume

VPNG = E / (GCVPNG × ηPNG)

Step 3 — Savings & Payback

Savings = CLPG − CPNG  •  Payback = CAPEX / Savings

  • mLPG = LPG consumption (kg/month), η = Burner thermal efficiency
  • GCVLPG ≈ 46.1 MJ/kg | GCVPNG ≈ 38 MJ/SCM (varies by region)
  • Typical burner efficiency: 0.80 – 0.90 for both fuels when properly tuned

Reference: Calorific Equivalency & Burner Efficiency Adjustment

PNG typically costs 30–50% less per unit of delivered heat than commercial LPG cylinders, but requires upfront piping and pressure regulation infrastructure. This tool isolates the fuel economics; always add local connection charges and gas utility deposits to the CAPEX figure for a full investment decision.
