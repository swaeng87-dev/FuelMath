Methodology & Formula

Off-grid industrial consumers often rely on LNG virtual pipelines — cryogenic tanker trucks delivering liquefied gas to on-site regasification units. When a grid pipeline reaches the area, the question becomes: does the delivered cost of piped gas beat the stacked LNG supply chain?

LNG Delivered Cost Stack

CLNG = Ccommodity + Ctrucking + Cregas

Monthly Cost Comparison

CostLNG = V × CLNG  •  CostPNG = V × CPNG

Savings

Savings = V × (CLNG − CPNG)

  • V = Monthly consumption (MMBtu) — all prices normalized to $/MMBtu
  • Commodity = LNG plant gate price (Henry Hub, JKM, or regional index)
  • Trucking = Cryogenic transport — scales with distance, road quality, and backhaul availability
  • Regasification = Ambient or heated vaporizer O&M at the consumer site

Additional Considerations

This tool captures fuel economics only. A full grid-connection decision should also weigh: pipeline connection CAPEX, utility deposit requirements, supply reliability (LNG trucking is weather/logistics dependent), and contract take-or-pay obligations on both sides.

Reference: Logistics Freighting & Regasification Terminal Factor

The virtual pipeline model is standard for industrial gas supply where grid infrastructure is absent or uneconomical. Typical LNG delivered costs range $12–$18/MMBtu depending on distance, while grid PNG in industrial zones often lands at $7–$11/MMBtu.
