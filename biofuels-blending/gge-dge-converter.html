Methodology & Formula

Alternative fuels are sold in wildly different units — SCM, kilograms, litres, kWh — making direct price and range comparison impossible. The Gasoline Gallon Equivalent (GGE) normalizes everything to the energy content of one gallon of gasoline.

Total Energy Content

E = Quantity × Energy per Unit (MJ)

GGE Conversion

GGE = E / 121.3  [MJ per gallon gasoline]

Gasoline Litre Equivalent

Leq = GGE × 3.785

  • 121.3 MJ = energy content of one US gallon of gasoline (the GGE basis, ≈ 33.7 kWh)
  • 3.785 L = one US gallon, used to express the result in litres
  • DGE (Diesel Gallon Equivalent) uses 137.4 MJ per gallon diesel — the same method with a larger basis
  • MPGe (Miles Per Gallon Equivalent) is built directly on GGE, so an EV's MPGe reflects this same 33.7 kWh-per-gallon figure

Energy vs. Drivetrain Efficiency

GGE compares fuel energy content only — it does not account for how efficiently a vehicle converts that energy to motion. Electric drivetrains (85–90% efficient) travel much farther per GGE than combustion engines (20–35%). For true cost-per-distance comparisons, pair GGE with the Fuel Cost per Kilometer tool.

Reference Standard: NIST Handbook 44 / Energy Equivalency Standards

NIST Handbook 44 governs weights and measures for fuel sales in the US and defines the GGE basis used at retail CNG and hydrogen stations, where fuel is legally sold by the gasoline-gallon equivalent. The EPA applies the same equivalencies for vehicle fuel-economy labeling (MPGe).
