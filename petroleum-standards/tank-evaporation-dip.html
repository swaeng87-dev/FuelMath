Methodology & Formula

Dipstick (dip-rod) measurements are the field-standard method for tank inventory verification. Converting a liquid height reading into volume depends on tank geometry — vertical cylinders are linear, but horizontal cylinders require circular segment integration because the cross-sectional area changes non-linearly with liquid height.

Vertical Cylinder

V = π × (D/2)² × h

Horizontal Cylinder (Partial Fill — Circular Segment)

V = L × [r²·cos⁻¹((r−h)/r) − (r−h)·√(2rh − h²)]

Rectangular Tank

V = Length × Width × h

Evaporation Loss Estimate

Vloss = V × (Evap Rate / 100)

  • h = Dip height (m), r = Tank radius (m), L = Tank length (m)
  • Horizontal cylinder volumes are non-linear — half the dip height does not equal half the volume
  • Evaporation losses occur in the ullage space (vapor headspace above the liquid) driven by temperature cycling and product volatility

Reference Standard: API Standard 2518

API Standard 2518 provides the methodology for estimating evaporation loss from fixed-roof tanks, accounting for tank dimensions, product vapor pressure, and ambient temperature cycling. For custody-grade inventory, apply temperature correction via the ASTM D1250 Corrector before mass accounting.
