Methodology & Formula

Volume-to-mass conversion is fundamental to petroleum custody transfer, inventory accounting, and transport planning. Since density varies by product grade and temperature, this tool applies standard density presets at the 15°C reference condition.

Step 1 — Unit Normalization to m³

V (m³) = V (L) × 0.001  •  V (m³) = V (US gal) × 0.00378541

Step 2 — Mass Calculation

m (kg) = V (m³) × ρ (kg/m³)

Step 3 — Metric Ton Conversion

m (MT) = m (kg) / 1000

  • 1 US gallon = 3.78541 litres = 0.00378541 m³
  • 1 m³ = 1,000 litres = 264.172 US gallons
  • Densities shown are mid-range values at 15°C; actual product density depends on refinery slate and seasonal blending
  • For temperature-corrected density, use the ASTM D1250 Corrector first, then feed the result here

Reference: Standard Petroleum Density Tables

Density presets align with published petroleum product specification ranges (EN 228 for petrol, EN 590 for diesel, DEF STAN 91-91 for Jet A1, ISO 8217 for fuel oils). For contract-grade precision, always use laboratory-measured density from the certificate of quality.
