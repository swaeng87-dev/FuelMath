Methodology & Formula

This calculator determines the physical mass of natural gas based on its volumetric flow or storage size at standard reference conditions.

m = V × ρ
  • m = Mass (kg)
  • V = Volume at standard conditions (SCM)
  • ρ = Density of the gas mixture (kg/m³)

Reference Standard: ISO 13443

Standard reference conditions for natural gas custody transfer and volumetric measurement are defined as 15°C (288.15 K) and 101.325 kPa (1 atm). Ensure your SCM volume is corrected to these base conditions before calculating mass.

Cubic Meter (CM) Vs Standard Cubic Meter (SCM)

The volume of gas is generally measured in CM (Cubic Meters). But the word 'Standard' is used when the volume varies due to composition or temperature. For the measurement of PNG (Piped Natural Gas), SCM (Standard Cubic Meter) is used. The composition of PNG differs from distributor to distributor, and from region to region. The main component of PNG is Methane. But it also contains Ethane, Propane, and Butane and incombustible gases like CO₂, N₂ and inert gases. PNG is 90% to 95% Methane. As there is no fixed Methane ratio and it flows through pipes in different temperature regions, calculating the gas in CM becomes difficult. So it is measured in SCM. It refers to cubic meters of gases at 15°C (288.15 K) temperature and 1 atmosphere pressure (101.325 kPa).

SCM to KG

As the PNG is measured in SCM, it becomes very difficult to convert it into KG. Generally, 1 SCM of Piped Natural Gas is about 0.71 to 0.80 kg. The difference is due to the composition and temperature of the gas. Pure Methane is approximately 0.71 kg in standard conditions. As Methane is lighter, the more Methane in the PNG, the lighter it is. For commercial PNG billing, you can consider 1 SCM of PNG is equivalent to 0.76 kg.

SCM to Metric Ton

If you want to convert SCM into Metric Tons (MT), you can safely divide it by 1000.

Metric Tons = kg ÷ 1000

For 1 SCM of PNG, if you take it as 0.76 kg, divide it by 1000 and you get 0.00076 MT.

0.76 ÷ 1000 = 0.00076

How to Calculate SCM to KG Manually

If your SCM value is not in the table below, you can easily calculate it using the standard conversion factor of 0.76. But before calculating the value, I would suggest checking your gas bill, if there is another value instead of 0.76.

Weight in KG = SCM × 0.76
  • Example 1: Convert 250 SCM to KG.
    250 × 0.76 = 190 KG
  • Example 2: Convert 450 SCM to KG.
    450 × 0.76 = 342 KG
  • Example 3: Convert 1,500 SCM to Metric Tons (MT).
    First, find KG: 1,500 × 0.76 = 1,140 KG. Then divide by 1000: 1,140 ÷ 1000 = 1.14 MT

SCM vs Sm³ vs Nm³

The main difference is in the temperature. SCM and Sm³ are basically the same. 'S' stands for 'Standard', while 'm³' is the abbreviation of Cubic Meter (CM). It refers to gas volume at 15°C (288.15 K) or sometimes 20°C, and 1 atmosphere (101.325 kPa) of pressure. But Nm³ is different. It stands for Normal Cubic Meter. It refers to gas volume at 0°C (273.15 K) and 1 atmosphere (101.325 kPa) of pressure.

It matters because gas volume differs at different temperatures. Gas at 0°C is denser than the gas at 15°C. For a rough conversion:

1 Nm³ ≈ 1.06 Sm³ (at 15°C)
