Methodology & Formula

This calculator determines the physical mass of natural gas based on its volumetric flow or storage size at standard reference conditions.

m = V × ρ
  • m = Mass (kg)
  • V = Volume at standard conditions (SCM)
  • ρ = Density of the gas mixture (kg/m³)

Reference Standard: ISO 13443

Standard reference conditions for natural gas custody transfer and volumetric measurement are defined as 15°C (288.15 K) and 101.325 kPa (1 atm). Ensure your SCM volume is corrected to these base conditions before calculating mass.

Cubic Meter Vs Standard Cubic Meter

The volume of gas is generally measured in CM (Cubic Meters). But the word 'Standard' is used when the volume varies due to composition or temperature. For the measurement of PNG (Piped Natural Gas), SCM (Standard Cubic Meter) is used. The composition of PNG differs from distributor to distributor, and from region to region. The main composition of PNG is Methane. But it also contains Ethane, Propane, and Butane. PNG is 90% to 95% Methane. As there is no fixed Methane ratio and it flows through pipes in different temperature regions, calculating the gas in CM becomes difficult. So it is measured in SCM.

SCM to KG

As the gas (PNG) is measured in SCM, it becomes very difficult to convert it into KG. Generally, 1 SCM is equivalent to 0.72 to 0.76 kg. The difference is due to the composition and temperature. The more Methane in the PNG, the lighter it is. For commercial PNG billing, 1 SCM is considered as 0.76 kg.
