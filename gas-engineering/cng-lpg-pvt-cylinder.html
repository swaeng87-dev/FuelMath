Methodology & Formula

The Real Gas Law extends the ideal gas equation with a compressibility correction factor Z, accounting for intermolecular forces at high cylinder pressures. For CNG at 200 bar, Z ≈ 0.85–0.95; ignoring this correction overestimates mass by 10–15%.

m = (P × V × M) / (Z × R × T)
  • m = Gas mass (kg)
  • P = Absolute pressure (Pa) — gauge reading × 100,000
  • V = Water capacity (m³) — liters ÷ 1,000
  • M = Molar mass (kg/mol) — g/mol ÷ 1,000
  • Z = Compressibility factor (CNG ≈ 0.9 at 200 bar, LPG vapor ≈ 0.95)
  • R = Universal gas constant = 8.314 J/(mol·K)
  • T = Absolute temperature (K) = °C + 273.15

Model Limitation

LPG cylinders contain liquid in equilibrium with vapor — this PVT model applies only to the vapor space or fully vaporized contents. For liquid LPG mass, use the liquid density directly (propane ≈ 0.493 kg/L at 25°C, butane ≈ 0.573 kg/L). This calculator is accurate for CNG and gaseous-phase LPG calculations.

Reference Standard: Real Gas Law (PVT)

The real gas law is the foundational thermodynamic equation for compressed gas inventory management, cylinder custody verification, and vehicle fuel state-of-charge estimation. For precise Z values at specific pressures, use the Z-Factor Calculator.
