Methodology & Formula

Gas utilities bill commercial and industrial customers based on the energy content of the gas delivered, not just the raw volume. This tool reverses that calculation to find the physical volume of gas that corresponds to your billed energy.

Volume (SCF) = (Energy × Conversion Factor) / GCV
  • 1 MMBtu = 1,000,000 BTU
  • 1 Therm = 100,000 BTU
  • 1 Gigajoule (GJ) ≈ 947,817 BTU
  • 1 SCM = 35.3147 SCF

Reference Standard: ISO 6976

ISO 6976 provides the standard methods for the calculation of calorific values, density, and Wobbe index of natural gases. Custody transfer contracts rely on these GCV conversions to settle billing disputes.
