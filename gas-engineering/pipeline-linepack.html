Methodology & Formula

Linepack is the total quantity of gas physically stored within a pressurized pipeline at any given moment. Gas dispatchers use linepack as a buffer to balance hourly demand fluctuations without adjusting upstream production. When demand spikes, linepack is "drawn down"; when demand falls, linepack is "built up".

Mean Pressure Integration

Pavg = (2/3) × [P₁ + P₂ − (P₁ × P₂) / (P₁ + P₂)]

Linepack Volume (Standard Conditions)

Vstd = Vgeo × (Pavg / Pb) × (Tb / T) × (1 / Z)

  • P₁, P₂ = Upstream and downstream absolute pressures (bar)
  • Vgeo = Geometric internal volume of pipe segment (m³)
  • Pb = 1.01325 bar (standard pressure), Tb = 288.15 K (15°C)
  • Z = Real gas compressibility factor (use Tool #7 for precision)

Reference: Mean Pressure Integration Method

The (2/3) weighting factor accounts for the non-linear pressure profile along a flowing pipeline. This formula assumes steady-state flow between the two measurement points. For transient conditions or complex network topologies, use computational hydraulic simulation software.
